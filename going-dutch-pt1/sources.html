Sources

Data used in the analyses

Dataset names link to the provider's download, data service or dataset page. The tables identify the editions used in this project. A live service may now supply a different edition.

PDOK is the Dutch government's geodata service. NDW is the national road-traffic data organization.

CC0 is a public-domain dedication. CC BY requires attribution, a license link and notice of changes. OpenStreetMap requires attribution and a notice of its license. Redistribution of adapted databases is subject to that license's share-alike terms.

The traffic-volume layers are subject to Dutch statutory reuse rules. Missing license statements do not by themselves prohibit reuse. Third-party rights remain undocumented.

Sources used for checks

The Fiets Telweek provider's original downloads are offline. The project obtained archived captures of those files.
